Summary

Backend engineer who loves distributed systems. I build the parts of software you never see but always rely on: the services, data and systems that keep things working quietly in the background. My focus is on how many machines work together as one, and how a system keeps going when some of them fail; right now I'm building a distributed key-value store from scratch to learn exactly that. I also enjoy building complete software, from the first idea to something people can use.
